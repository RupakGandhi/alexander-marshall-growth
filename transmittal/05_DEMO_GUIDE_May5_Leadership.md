## 🤔 Questions they will probably ask — and your answers

### Q1. *"What happens when a teacher disagrees with a score?"*
**A:** *"The acknowledgement signature is acknowledgement, not agreement — same as a paper observation. You can add a 'teacher response' textbox below the published observation in 30 minutes if you want one. Easy add."*

### Q2. *"Can I observe without scoring everything?"*
**A:** *"Yes. Score the indicators you focused on. The platform doesn't force a complete rubric for every observation. Drop-in, formative, walk-through — all supported by simply scoring the 1–5 indicators that came up."*

### Q3. *"What about non-evaluative coaching observations?"*
**A:** *"Use the 'draft' status and never publish. The coach can review, the teacher never sees it, and it's still pinned to the teacher's record for trend analysis."*

### Q4. *"What if our policy changes — say, we adopt a new rubric in 2027?"*
**A:** *"The Pedagogy Library and PD modules are CSV-portable. Domains, indicators, levels, descriptors — all editable. We've already done this once: in April we updated B.d, B.e, and E.c content district-wide via CSV. Took us 12 minutes."*

### Q5. *"Who owns the data?"*
**A:** *"You do. It lives in a Cloudflare D1 database we provisioned for you, named `alexander-marshall-growth-production`. We can hand you full export at any time, or hand you the keys entirely."*

### Q6. *"What's the privacy story?"*
**A:** *"Bcrypt-hashed passwords, no third-party tracking, no AI vendor sees teacher or student data — the feedback generator is rule-based, pulling from your own Pedagogy Library. FERPA-compatible because no student records are stored unless you choose to add them."*

### Q7. *"What happens if a principal scripts notes for an hour and the laptop dies?"*
**A:** *"Auto-save on every keystroke writes to Cloudflare's edge within 200ms. They lose at most one keystroke. Open the URL again on any device — same observation, same notes."*

### Q8. *"How is this different from Rocky Mountain?"*
**A:** *"Rocky Mountain is generic content for many districts. This is the **Marshall framework you already use**, the **120 PD modules tied to your specific indicators**, and your district's **voice and tone** in every starter sentence. Plus integrated observations + reporting that Rocky Mountain doesn't do at all."*

### Q9. *"Can teachers see other teachers' observations?"*
**A:** *"No. A teacher sees only their own published record. Principals see only their assigned teachers. Coaches see only PD reviews assigned to them. The superintendent sees aggregates and roll-ups, not individual scripted notes."*

### Q10. *"What's our buy-in cost? What's the upkeep?"*
**A:** *"$10K full platform / year or $5K Chapter edition (proposal is in the package). Upkeep is in your hands — every update path (rubric, pedagogy, PD modules, users) is a button or a CSV. We're available for support, not gatekeeping."*

### Q11. *"What about union / contract concerns?"*
**A:** *"Same observations you do today on paper, just digital. Acknowledgement signature, teacher's right to comment, principal's documentation — all preserved. Many districts find their union likes this **better** because the teacher sees the **structured** feedback (glows, grows, focus areas, next steps), not just an evaluator's opinion."*

### Q12. *"Can we pilot before signing?"*
**A:** *"You're already piloting. The site is live with your real staff list, real Marshall framework, real 120 PD modules. The May 5 demo is from your production system. Use it through the rest of the school year — we collect feedback, you collect data, decide in August."*

### Q13. *"What if Aaron and Shannon want different things?"*
**A:** *"They'll get the same toolkit, but the data they each see is filtered to their own building. Plus the super admin can scope settings per-building. Same platform, different lenses."*

### Q14. *"What about teachers who aren't tech-savvy?"*
**A:** *"Pamela's flow we just walked through took us 90 seconds. There's no software to install, no training video to watch first. The blue banner on first sign-in is the whole 'tutorial.' If a teacher gets stuck, the principal sees them stuck (status = 'started' but no progress) and can intervene — which is the coaching point anyway."*

### Q15. *"Can we white-label this for other districts?"*
**A:** *"Yes. The platform is built to support multiple districts, and the staff seed file shows how a new district would be onboarded. Hand-shake conversation, separately."*
